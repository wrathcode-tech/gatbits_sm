Help & Support

Welcome to the GTBSFLICKSY Support Center. We're here to help you make the most of your experience. If you're having trouble with the app or have questions about our features, please review the information below.

1. Account & Login

Manage your account settings and resolve access issues:

  • Changing your password: Go to Settings > Change Password.
  • Forgot password: Use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login screen to reset via email or phone.
  • Account security: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for extra protection.

2. Content & Creation

Learn how to share your moments with the community:

  • Posting: Tap the '+' icon to upload photos or videos to your feed.
  • Reels: Create short, engaging videos by navigating to the Reels tab.
  • Stories: Share temporary updates that disappear after 24 hours.

3. Privacy & Safety

We prioritize your safety and control over your data:

  • Private Account: Toggle 'Private Account' in Settings to control who sees your posts.
  • Blocking: You can block users by visiting their profile and selecting 'Block'.
  • Reporting: Report inappropriate content or behavior using the 'Report' option on any post.

4. Technical Support

Having technical difficulties? Try these steps:

  • Ensure you are using the latest version of the GTBSFLICKSY app.
  • Check your internet connection (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data).
  • Clear app cache or restart your device if you encounter glitches.

5. Contact Us

If your issue is not resolved, our dedicated support team is ready to assist you. Please reach out to us via email:
