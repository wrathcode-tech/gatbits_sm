Delete Your FLICKSY Account

How to Request Account Deletion

At FLICKSY, we respect your privacy and provide you full control over your account. If you would like to permanently delete your FLICKSY account, you can submit a deletion request by contacting our support team.

To request account deletion, please send an email using your registered email address or mention your registered mobile number so that we can verify your identity.

Email your request to:

support@gatbits.com

Please include the following details in your email request:

  • Your registered email address or mobile number
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • A short confirmation that you want to permanently delete your FLICKSY account

What Happens After Your Request

Once we receive your request, our support team will review and verify your identity to ensure that the request is submitted by the rightful account owner.

After verification, your account will be permanently deleted from the FLICKSY platform.

Important Information

Please note that account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. Once your account is deleted, you will lose access to:

  • Your account login credentials
  • Your profile and uploaded content
  • Your activity and account history
  • Any rewards or data associated with your account

If you decide to use FLICKSY again in the future, you will need to create a new account.

If you are facing any issues with your account, we recommend contacting our support team before requesting deletion. Our team may be able to help resolve your issue without deleting your account.

For any assistance or to submit your account deletion request, please contact us at support@gatbits.com.
