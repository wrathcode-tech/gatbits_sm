Child Safety Standards

GTBS Flicksy is committed to maintaining a safe and secure platform for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and strictly prohibit any content or behavior that may harm minors.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

We strictly prohibit:

  • Any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Content that exploits or endangers minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or inappropriate interaction involving minors

Any violation will result in immediate removal of content, account suspension, and may be reported to relevant law enforcement authorities.

2. User Safety Measures

To protect our community, we have implemented:

  • 🚨 In-app reporting system to report harmful or suspicious content
  • 🔍 Content moderation (manual + automated where applicable)
  • 🚫 Strict community guidelines to prevent misuse
  • ⛔ User blocking and restriction features
3. Reporting & Response

Users can report content or accounts directly within the app. Once reported:

  • We review reports promptly
  • Take appropriate action (content removal / account ban)
  • Maintain confidentiality of reporters

For urgent concerns, users can also contact us at:
support@gatbits.com

4. Compliance with Laws

We comply with all applicable child safety laws and regulations and cooperate fully with:

  • Law enforcement agencies
  • Regulatory authorities

We report confirmed CSAM cases to relevant national and international authorities as required.

5. Age Restrictions

Our platform is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirement as per applicable laws. We do not knowingly allow minors to engage in unsafe or restricted activities.

6. Continuous Improvement

We continuously improve our safety systems by:

  • Updating moderation practices
  • Monitoring emerging risks
  • Enhancing reporting and detection tools
7. Contact Information

If you have any concerns regarding child safety or CSAE compliance, please contact:

support@gatbits.com
